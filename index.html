Good to know

The questions people ask.

Is my data private?

Yes. Kepto has no account, no ads, and no tracking, and no Retriever Labs servers ever receive your data. Your receipts, photos, and documents are processed and stored on your device.

How much does Kepto cost?

Kepto is free for up to 25 receipts. Kepto+ is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks unlimited items, a photo on every receipt, unlimited vault documents, exports, service reminders, custom categories, and advanced stats.

Does Kepto need the internet?

No. Scanning, parsing, reminders, and everything else run on your device, so Kepto works fully offline. On iPhone, iCloud Sync uses your connection when it's available. On Android the app requests no internet permission at all.

Is Kepto on Android?

Yes. Kepto runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, and you'll find it on the App Store and Google Play.

I got a new phone. Will my data come back?

Yes, if iCloud Sync (iPhone) or Back up to Google (Android) was on. Sign into the same Apple ID or Google account and your receipts return. Vault documents are never restored on a new device.

Can I get my vault documents back if I lose my phone?

No. The vault's encryption key never leaves your phone and is never backed up, so the documents can't be recovered by you or by us. Keep your own copy of anything important; you can export any document from the vault.
