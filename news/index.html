November 2026

iPhone 7.1, Android 1.6
  • Receipts, Bills and Subscriptions each have their own tab and page
  • Add anything from one screen: scan, photo, PDF or type it in
  • Share a receipt photo or PDF into Kepto from any app
  • Extra warranties, return windows and document expiry reminders
  • Tax claims with work use, and export as folders

Early November 2026

iPhone 7.0, Android 1.5
  • Three home screen widgets: warranties, next bill and spending
  • A Spending card in Stats: purchases, bills and subscriptions
  • Your own categories get their own filter and Stats row
  • Groceries, meals and fuel are filed without a warranty

Late October 2026

iPhone 6.9, Android 1.4
  • Scan a bill and Kepto tracks it, with a reminder before it is due
  • Track subscriptions, with renewal and free trial reminders
  • Group receipts by month, category, room, store or warranty
  • Scanned IDs go straight to the Document Vault
