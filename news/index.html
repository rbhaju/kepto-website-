Coming soon

Kepto 7.6 for iPhone and iPad · Kepto 1.12 for Android. Both are with the stores for review.

Bills that fit how you are billed
  • A bill or subscription can repeat fortnightly, every 2 or 6 months, or every few weeks or months that you choose.
  • A bill can be a one-off. It has one due date, shows as overdue until you mark it paid, and then it is done.
  • "How often?" is now one row with a menu, so the form is shorter.
  • The scanner recognises fortnightly bills and two-month billing periods.
Paying bills
  • Mark as paid is there straight away. You no longer have to pick a payment method first.
  • A direct debit bill is never shown as overdue, since it pays itself.
  • Changing a bill's due date keeps what you have already marked as paid.
  • Picking a payment method on an old bill no longer makes it overdue at once.
  • Once a bill is marked paid, its page, the Bills list and Home show the next date still to pay.
  • A paid one-off bill is filed under Paid.
Receipts and the vault
  • Add a link to a product's manual on its receipt, under Details. A Manual row then appears on the receipt and opens it.
  • The Document Vault takes PDFs of up to 40 pages. It was 10.
  • The receipt page has one Share button, where it had two.
Hand-drawn icons
  • A new Icons choice in Settings, Appearance: Line or Hand-drawn. It covers the navigation bar, the add button and the buttons at the top of a page.
  • If you already use Kepto, nothing changes until you choose it. New installs start with Hand-drawn.

23 November 2026

Kepto 7.5 for iPhone and iPad · Kepto 1.11 for Android

A new look, if you want it
  • A Compact receipt page: the summary at the top, then Receipt, Notes, Reminders and Details as rows that open in place. Classic is still there in Settings, Appearance.
  • A floating navigation bar, also in Appearance. New installs start with both; if you already use Kepto, nothing changes until you choose it.
  • The same hand-drawn icons across more of the app.
Reminders in one place
  • One Reminders section on each receipt shows the warranty reminder with its date, your service reminder and any reminders you added. The row shows the next date at a glance.
  • Add a reminder while you are filling in a receipt, without saving first.
  • One "Remind me" setting now also decides what counts as expiring soon.
  • "Warranty reminders" in Settings is now called "Expiry reminders", because it also covers return windows and vault documents.
Less going to Edit
  • Type a note straight on the receipt page.
  • Attach a receipt photo or PDF straight from the receipt page.
  • The add and edit forms show their optional parts as closed rows, so you can see what is there.
Scanning
  • Refund dockets are filed as refunds, with no warranty.
  • Services, such as a lab test or a delivery fee, are filed with no warranty.
  • More Australian receipts are recognised as Australian dollars.
Document Vault
  • Kepto can read the number and expiry date from the machine-readable lines of a passport or ID card. They are only used when their built-in check digits agree.
  • It understands the 4a, 4b and 5 field codes on driving licences, "Valid to", and dates given as a month and year.
  • A scanned number is still only offered for you to check. Reading happens on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere.

Late November 2026

Kepto 7.4 for iPhone and iPad · Kepto 1.10 for Android

Bills and subscriptions
  • Search bills and subscriptions and use the status filters to find active, overdue or cancelled items where applicable.
  • Select several bills or subscriptions and move them together.
  • Record how you pay a bill, mark it paid and see whether you paid early, on time or late. Unpaid bills show when they are overdue.
Receipts and Stats
  • Tap the other-currencies note in Stats to find the receipts excluded from your main-currency total.
  • Improved scanning for receipts and bills, including more ways that printed amounts and dates can appear.
Document Vault
  • Import documents from Photos or Files and review the details Kepto finds, including issue dates and document numbers when it can read them.
  • Open a scan full screen and pinch to zoom in.
Exports
  • Improved PDF, CSV and folder ZIP exports for receipts.
  • Choose Tax claims only in date range export and select PDF for a report of the items marked for tax.

November 2026

iPhone 7.3 and Android 1.8 and 1.9: hand-drawn icons, a Cards setting, receipts split into their items, and a vault that syncs through iCloud on iPhone. 7.2 didn't reach the App Store, so on iPhone and iPad, 7.3 also brings everything in the 7.2 notes below.

Receipts with several items
  • Receipts and PDF invoices with several items split into separate items, one tick per item
  • Reads table layouts like "$34.00 x1 $34.00", including prices in columns on PDF invoices
  • PDF invoices are read across every page
  • If a PDF's items don't add up to its total, Kepto still lists them and tells you the difference
  • Bills printed with whole-number prices are read item by item
  • On Android, this arrives in 1.9
Hand-drawn icons
  • New hand-drawn icons across the app, in your theme's own colours
  • In Settings, empty states, categories, cards and widgets
  • 40 icons to choose from for your own categories
Cards, your way
  • A Cards setting in Appearance: full cards, cards led by their photo, or compact rows
  • Tap a card's photo to open it full screen
Document Vault
  • iPhone and iPad: documents sync through your own iCloud, encrypted on the device first
  • Each card shows a preview of the first page; tap it to open the pages
  • Edit a document: its name, type, number, expiry and pages
  • Share or download any page or photo from the full-screen viewer
  • Scan an ID & certificate and Kepto guesses the document type
  • Filter by All, Expiring soon or Expired
Widgets
  • Track one, a new widget that follows a single warranty, return window or bill
  • Arrows to step through Warranties ending and Bills & subscriptions
Also new
  • A clearer Settings, with a page for each section
  • Kepto's own time picker for reminders
  • Search puts the best match first and finds archived receipts too
  • Android: Kepto's own camera, which finds the receipt's edges and cleans up the scan, as on iPhone
  • Android: swipe actions and the Quick Settings scan tile use the new icons
  • A bigger crown on the Kepto+ card, and swipe through its lines

Mid November 2026

iPhone 7.2 and Android 1.7: a rebuilt scanner, one-tap scanning, and bills kept month by month.

Scanning, rebuilt (iPhone)
  • A new camera finds the receipt's edges as you aim and outlines them in gold
  • Drag any corner to fix the crop, or rotate the photo, before you use it
  • Clean up gives a scanner look: even light, white paper and dark ink
  • Long receipts: add page after page, then tap Done
  • Pick a photo from your library right from the camera
Scan in one tap
  • iPhone: add "Scan a receipt" to Control Center or the Lock Screen
  • Android: add the "Scan a receipt" tile to Quick Settings
  • Hold the Kepto app icon and tap "Scan a receipt"
  • Hold the + button to go straight to the camera
Bills, month by month
  • Keep each month's bill on its page, with a photo
  • Turn on a reminder to add each new bill when it is due
  • Past bills are included in your backup and restore
Organise your way
  • Sub-categories under any category, like Home 1 then Water bill
  • Name your own rooms, beside the built-in ones
  • Name a warranty, for example Manufacturer or Extended
  • PDF and folder exports include sub-categories
Also new
  • A fresh look, with new icons and a coverage ring on Home
  • Stats month by month or year by year
  • Dashed or solid lines, and round or square buttons
  • Your own reminders on anything, at the time you choose
  • A tidier Settings, with one section open at a time
  • Send feedback straight from Settings
  • Fixes for small iPhones and iPad
