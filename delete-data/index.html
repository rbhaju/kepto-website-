Your data

Delete your data.

Kepto has no accounts, so there is nothing for us to close.

Other apps have a "delete your account" button. Kepto can't, because it never had your account or your data in the first place. Here is how to remove everything yourself, wherever it lives.

The short version
  • There is no Kepto account to delete, and we hold none of your data.
  • Deleting the app removes everything on that phone, including the vault.
  • If sync or backup was on, also remove the copy from your own iCloud or Google account.
  • Kepto+ stays with your Apple or Google account. There is nothing to cancel.
  • To leave Kepto news, use the link in any of our emails, or ask us.

What we hold

Nothing. Kepto has no account and no servers of ours, so there is no account to close and no copy of your receipts, photos or documents for us to delete. Your data exists only in the places below, and you control all of them.

Delete items inside Kepto

In the Home or Items list, swipe a receipt left or press and hold it, then choose Delete. Kepto asks first, then removes the item and its receipt photos for good.

Choosing Archive instead moves the item to Settings, Archived, where it is deleted automatically after 30 days. You can empty the archive sooner with Delete all, or open an archived item and delete it there.

On iPhone

With iCloud Sync on, an item you delete is also removed from your iCloud and from your other iPhones and iPads.

Remove everything from your phone

Delete the Kepto app. That removes every receipt, photo, bill, subscription and setting stored on that device, along with everything in the Document Vault. Vault documents cannot be recovered afterwards, so export anything you still need first.

Remove your iCloud copy

On iPhone

If iCloud Sync was on, a copy lives in your own private iCloud and would come back if you reinstalled Kepto. To remove it, open the iPhone's Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then the storage management screen, choose Kepto, and delete its data from iCloud. Apple holds that copy, not us, so only you can remove it.

Remove your Google backup

On Android

If Back up to Google was on, Kepto's data is part of your phone's backup in your own Google account. You can delete that device backup from Google Drive under Storage, Backups, or by turning backup off in your phone's settings. This removes the backup for your other apps on that phone as well. Google holds that backup, not us, so only you can remove it.

Backup files and exports

Any backup file, PDF or CSV you exported from Kepto sits wherever you saved or sent it, such as Files, Drive or email. Delete those copies there.

Messages you sent us

The one thing we do receive is a message you choose to send through the Support form or by email. Ask us at support@kepto.com.au and we will delete that correspondence.

Kepto news emails

If you joined Kepto news, tap the link to leave at the bottom of any of our emails and your address is removed at once. You can also ask at support@kepto.com.au and we will remove it for you.

Your Kepto+ purchase

Kepto+ is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple or Google account, not to us. There is nothing to cancel, and deleting your data does not remove the purchase. You can restore it if you ever install Kepto again.
