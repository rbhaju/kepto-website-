Mid November 2026

iPhone 7.2 and Android 1.7: a rebuilt scanner, one-tap scanning, and bills kept month by month.

Scanning, rebuilt (iPhone)
  • A new camera finds the receipt's edges as you aim and outlines them in gold
  • Drag any corner to fix the crop, or rotate the photo, before you use it
  • Clean up gives a scanner look: even light, white paper and dark ink
  • Long receipts: add page after page, then tap Done
  • Pick a photo from your library right from the camera
Scan in one tap
  • iPhone: add "Scan a receipt" to Control Center or the Lock Screen
  • Android: add the "Scan a receipt" tile to Quick Settings
  • Hold the Kepto app icon and tap "Scan a receipt"
  • Hold the + button to go straight to the camera
Bills, month by month
  • Keep each month's bill on its page, with a photo
  • Turn on a reminder to add each new bill when it is due
  • Past bills are included in your backup and restore
Organise your way
  • Sub-categories under any category, like Home 1 then Water bill
  • Name your own rooms, beside the built-in ones
  • Name a warranty, for example Manufacturer or Extended
  • PDF and folder exports include sub-categories
Also new
  • A fresh look, with new icons and a coverage ring on Home
  • Stats month by month or year by year
  • Dashed or solid lines, and round or square buttons
  • Your own reminders on anything, at the time you choose
  • A tidier Settings, with one section open at a time
  • Send feedback straight from Settings
  • Fixes for small iPhones and iPad

November 2026

iPhone 7.1, Android 1.6
  • Receipts, Bills and Subscriptions each have their own tab and page
  • Add anything from one screen: scan, photo, PDF or type it in
  • Share a receipt photo or PDF into Kepto from any app
  • Extra warranties, return windows and document expiry reminders
  • Tax claims with work use, and export as folders

Early November 2026

iPhone 7.0, Android 1.5
  • Three home screen widgets: warranties, next bill and spending
  • A Spending card in Stats: purchases, bills and subscriptions
  • Your own categories get their own filter and Stats row
  • Groceries, meals and fuel are filed without a warranty

Late October 2026

iPhone 6.9, Android 1.4
  • Scan a bill and Kepto tracks it, with a reminder before it is due
  • Track subscriptions, with renewal and free trial reminders
  • Group receipts by month, category, room, store or warranty
  • Scanned IDs go straight to the Document Vault
