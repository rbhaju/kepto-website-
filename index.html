How it works

From a crumpled receipt to covered.

1

Snap the receipt.

3

Kepto counts the days and sends one calm reminder a few weeks before each warranty ends.

Good to know

The questions people ask.

Is my data private?

Yes. Kepto has no account, no ads, and no tracking, and no Retriever Labs servers ever receive your data. Your receipts, photos, and documents are processed and stored on your device.

How much does Kepto cost?

Kepto is free for up to 25 receipts. Kepto+ is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks unlimited items, a photo on every receipt, unlimited vault documents, exports, service reminders, custom categories, and advanced stats.

Does Kepto need the internet?

No. Scanning, parsing, reminders, and everything else run on your device, so Kepto works fully offline. On iPhone, iCloud Sync uses your connection when it's available. On Android the app requests no internet permission at all.

Is Kepto on Android?

Yes. Kepto runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, and you'll find it on the App Store and Google Play.

I got a new phone. Will my data come back?

Yes, if iCloud Sync (iPhone) or Back up to Google (Android) was on. Sign into the same Apple ID or Google account and your receipts return. Vault documents are never restored on a new device.

Can I get my vault documents back if I lose my phone?

No. The vault's encryption key never leaves your phone and is never backed up, so the documents can't be recovered by you or by us. Keep your own copy of anything important; you can export any document from the vault.

Who makes Kepto

Built after losing the receipts.

I'm Rohan from Retriever Labs, and I made Kepto. I lived in Sydney for eight years and worked in IT, including at NSW Ambulance. When it was time to move home to Kathmandu, I had to sell almost everything I owned, and that's when the receipts went missing: some deleted from my email, some lost when I changed phones, some cleared to free up storage. It made a stressful time even harder.

I looked for an app to keep receipts and track warranties, and every one had the same three problems:

  1. About $40 a year. A subscription to store a few receipts, in an app you might open twice a year.
  2. Sign up first. An account before you can do anything.
  3. Your data on their servers. What you bought, where, when and for how much. That's a detailed picture of someone's life, and handing it over felt wrong.

So I built the app I wanted: pay once, no account, and everything stays on your phone. I started Kepto in Sydney and keep building it from Nepal.

You don't have to trust where I am.There's no account and no sign-up, and your receipts stay on your phone and in your own iCloud or Google backup. I don't run a server that holds them, so there's nothing of yours for me to see.
